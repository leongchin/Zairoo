Still have questions?

Is the 7-day learning really free?

Yes. Your child gets 7 days of full premium access. If you don't continue, the account moves to the free Basic plan and you pay nothing.

Will Zai just give my child the answers?

No. Zai works in tutor mode: it asks guiding questions and gives hints so your child works out each step.

Which syllabus do the questions follow?

The Singapore MOE Primary Mathematics syllabus for P1 to P6, with questions in the style of past-year school exam papers, curated by former MOE teachers and educators.

How much is it after the free 7 days?

S$21 per child per month. You can also stay on the free Basic plan with limited papers and hints.
